Our Heritage

Mama's Kitchen,
Bukka Style, With Class

Mama Africa Bukka Hub was born from one woman's kitchen and a simple belief — that real Nigerian native food deserves a proper home, and a proper crowd to enjoy it with. Every pot of black soup, every mound of amala, every bowl of bush meat soup is prepared the way Mama has always done it — slow, seasoned right, and served with love.

We're more than a restaurant. We're a bukka, a bakery, and a bush bar all in one — the local African chilling spot where you come for the food, stay for the pepper soup and asun, and never miss a match.
